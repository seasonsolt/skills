Write a humorous first-person essay of 450-650 words from the fictional material below. Do not copy sentences from any published work. Do not add facts, numbers, people or quotations beyond the material.

Material: The narrator, aged fifty-one, bought a motorized "smart" standing desk on his doctor's advice. It cost 640 dollars. His neighbor Mrs. Abernathy, a retired schoolteacher, fixed the desk by pulling the plug out and putting it back. The narrator now works at the desk sitting down, at its lowest setting.
